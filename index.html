ABOUT PEN VENTURES

PEN Ventures — Pioneer, Explorer, Navigator

PEN Ventures aims to discover and support promising early-stage startups to help them explore and pioneer new overseas markets for their continuous growth and diversification of the Korean startup ecosystem.
We hope to be their SHERPA and SUPPORTER.

Bring to light new promising companies

Discover early-stage startups with potential to conduct direct investment with the purpose of growing with the company.

Operate accelerating programs

Operate programs such as DIPS 1000+ and Baby Unicorn 200 to help companies expand globally and attract investment.

Build a Global Collaborative Network

Provide a wide network of the world’s leading companies and investors to support the global expansion of companies.

Elaborate collaboration projects

Conduct collaborative projects with domestic and foreign conglomerates to support global entry and new opportunities.
